This timeline presents a comparative overview of topic attention, by data source (news and social media).

Based on the custom taxonomy developed for 117 topics of interest to FEWS NET, the default timeline displays the aggregated Attention metric over time for each data stream. By default, when no topic is filtered, the visualization displays an average record-level attention rate for all topics, for each data stream, per day.

Attention is based on record presence: the share of Telegram posts or news stories in the selected period where a topic is detected at least once. Scale: 0 to 1.
