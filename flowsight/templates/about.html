About FlowSight

Human mobility, understood as voluntary and involuntary movements of people, often driven by factors such as conflict, economic instability, or climate change, presents complex challenges for humanitarian response and policy planning. Timely, accurate insights into mobility patterns and emerging dynamics are essential to effectively anticipate needs, coordinate aid, and mitigate risks.

FlowSight: Human Mobility Multimodal Dashboard is a tool focused on displacement and migration, designed to ingest, process, analyze, and visualize large-scale, near real-time data streams from diverse digital sources such as social media, news outlets, and search engines.

How FlowSight Works

The tool harnesses diverse, high-frequency digital data streams to create structured, AI-ready datasets that capture global and local patterns of human mobility. This enables detection of mobility-related developments and supports advanced, data-driven insight for humanitarian action and policy planning.

Our approach builds on Digital Methods, specifically online issue mapping, to identify, analyze, and visualize emerging topics and concerns within digital environments.

Unlike traditional human mobility data reliant on slower reporting cycles, digital platforms provide real-time issue traces such as text, images, hyperlinks, and metadata that can be systematically collected and analyzed to deliver timely situational awareness for humanitarian and mobility decision-making.

This dashboard aims to serve humanitarian actors, researchers, and policy makers by providing ethically sourced insights that support crisis anticipation, hotspot detection, needs forecasting, and disinformation monitoring. The pilot version is focused on Sudan, with additional countries envisioned in future iterations.

FlowSight draws on three core dimensions. First, Social Listening on public Telegram groups and channels extracts high-frequency public discourse, capturing community narratives, grassroots coordination, and sentiment trends. Additional context is provided through ingestion of local online news media stories. Aggregation of search behavior data through Google Trends supports identification of shifts in public concern, interest, or awareness related to displacement and migration crises.

Together, these streams form a multimodal framework that delivers innovative pathways for understanding human mobility.
